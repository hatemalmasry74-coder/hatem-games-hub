## Full description (≤ 4000)
```
Moody & Aboody are two brothers with a new adventure every day — and your child plays along, learns and has fun.

An Arabic (Egyptian-dialect) game for children aged 6 to 12: puzzles, mini-games and short stories that grow good habits and values without preaching.

What is inside
• Many adventures: home, school, the neighbourhood, the market, the park and club, a sea trip, grandma's house, travelling to dad, "my daily habits", the family gathering and an adventure race [check the count in the final build].
• Different kinds of play: drag and drop, memory, sequencing, measuring, choices, and running and jumping.
• Two levels: easier questions for Aboody (6) and harder ones for Moody (9) — children can switch.
• A real human voice reads the instructions and questions, so children who cannot read yet can play alone.
• Stars, coins and stickers, and a dressing room to dress the characters.
• Parents' corner: a summary of the values and skills practised, plus sound, level and name settings.

For parents
• No ads at all.
• No accounts, no chat, no links that take a child out of the game.
• Works offline after installation.
• We collect no personal data; progress is stored on the device only.
• Any purchase asks for a parent check first.

Free or not? (plainly)
The app is free to download and the first mission of every adventure is completely free. The remaining missions are unlocked with the "Moody & Aboody Premium" subscription ([monthly] or [yearly]). Google Play shows the price before you confirm. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it; you can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. If you cancel, the free missions and your child's progress stay on the device.

Privacy policy: [URL]
Questions and suggestions: [EMAIL]
```

## Notes
- Play has no keyword field; the natural words in the text (kids, Arabic, puzzles, adventures, educational) are enough. Do not repeat words or name other games.
- Category: Games → Educational (or Adventure).
- Do not put "free" alone in the title, icon or graphics; say "no ads" instead.
- Screenshots are produced from the real game (`docs/play/assets/out/`); no photos of real children.
