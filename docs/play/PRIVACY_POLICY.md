# Privacy Policy — Moody & Aboody (مودي وعبودي)

_Last updated: [DATE — fill in before publishing]_

## 1. In short

**We do not collect any personal information.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. Everything the game remembers (stars, coins, stickers, settings, and any names a parent types) is stored only on your device and never sent to us or anyone else. The only third-party component is **Google Play Billing**, which handles the optional subscription.

## 2. Who we are

The app “Moody & Aboody” (مودي وعبودي) is published by **[DEVELOPER NAME]**. Contact: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## 3. Who the app is for

The app is designed for children (roughly 5–12 years old) and their parents. It is built to follow Google Play’s Families Policy and to respect the principles of COPPA (United States), GDPR and GDPR-K (EU/UK, Article 8), the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (No. 151 of 2020) and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. Because we collect no personal data, most obligations in those laws are met by design.

## 4. What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect or receive: names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, videos, voice recordings, contacts, files, advertising IDs, device identifiers, IP addresses or usage statistics. The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

## 5. What is stored on your device

To let a child continue where they stopped, the game keeps on the device: progress (finished missions, stars, coins, stickers), settings (sound, difficulty, graphics), outfit choices, and — if a parent chooses to personalise the game in the family setup or the Parents’ corner — the first names or nicknames and simple looks (hair, clothes colour) of the players. This information stays inside the app’s private storage. It is not uploaded, not backed up to the cloud (Android backup is switched off) and not shared.

You can erase it at any time: Parents’ corner → “Reset all progress”, or uninstall the app, or clear the app’s storage in Android settings.

## 6. Internet use

The game works fully offline: all pictures, sounds and the recorded voice lines are inside the app. The app’s own code makes **no network requests** (we checked the code for fetch/WebSocket/XMLHttpRequest/external URLs: it only reads its own bundled files). There are no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting libraries, no social features and no links that take a child out of the app.

Motion/orientation sensors may be read on the device only to tilt the background slightly; the values are never stored or sent. The screen is kept awake while the game is open.

## 7. Subscription and payments

Some content is free (one free mission in each adventure). A subscription unlocks the rest. Purchases are made **only through Google Play**. Google processes the payment under its own terms and [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we never see or store card or account details. We receive only anonymous sales reports from Google Play Console. The price, billing period and renewal terms are shown by Google Play before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; you can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. A parental check is shown inside the app before any purchase screen.

## 8. Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or tracking. The only third-party component is the Google Play Billing library supplied by Google for the subscription.

## 9. Security

Because no personal data leaves the device, there is nothing for us to protect on a server. The app does not load remote code or remote web pages and blocks unencrypted (http) traffic.

## 10. Your rights and parents’ choices

We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You control the on-device data as described above. If you have a question or believe we hold data about you, write to **[CONTACT EMAIL]** and we will answer.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example a new feature that needs data), we will update this page and the Play Store “Data safety” section before the change ships, and ask a parent for consent where the law requires.
